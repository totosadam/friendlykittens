HOW IT WORKS

From first phone call to go-home day

STEP 01

Call or email

Tell us a little about your household and what you are hoping for. There is no cost to start the conversation, and we will tell you honestly what is available.

STEP 02

Book a visit

Come to the house in Waterford by appointment and meet the litter and their parents. Sit on the floor and let a kitten choose you.

STEP 03

Reserve your kitten

When you have found the one, we hold them for you and keep you posted with photos while they finish growing up with their mother.

STEP 04

Bring them home

At eight to ten weeks your kitten goes home with its first shots and its adoption packet, and we stay a phone call away for life.
