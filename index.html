OUR PROMISE

What responsible breeding means to us

Raising kittens is not a side business for us. It is something our family has done carefully for over 31 years, and every decision starts with the same question: is this best for the cats and for the family who will love them?

Here is the standard we hold ourselves to with every litter.

Ask us anything →

A genetic guarantee

Our kittens are guaranteed free of genetic defects and are PKD negative, so there is nothing debilitating waiting down the road.

Home, not a cattery floor

Our cats and kittens live in the house with us. That is the single biggest reason ours settle so quickly into a new home.

Daily handling

Kittens are held, brushed, and played with every day from the first week, and meet the ordinary noise of family life early.

A wellness check at sale

Every kitten goes through a wellness checkup before it leaves, so you can see for yourself you are getting a healthy, well loved cat.

Meet them in person

We would rather you visit, sit on the floor, and let a kitten choose you than pick one from a photograph.

A home for life

If your circumstances ever change, come back to us first. No kitten of ours should end up in a shelter.

MEET THE BREEDER

Hi, I'm Sean, and my family has a passion for Himalayans

One of the area's only Himalayan and Persian breeders in Northern Virginia, just outside Leesburg.

I have been raising cats and kittens for over 31 years, specialising in Himalayan and Persian cats. I take great pride in the home and the nurturing I give them, and I believe raising kittens in my house rather than in a cage or a crate is what makes them so people-friendly: less shy, less frightened in a new home, and far more compatible with the pets already there.

All of our kittens are hand raised with lots of love and TLC. They grow up around people, kids and other animals, so they are ready to venture into your home from day one. They do very well as lap cats and with small children, and they are not shy or unfriendly. We raise them as part of our family, not just as pets.

I work with a well established clinic in Purcellville, VA — Blue Ridge Vets. Friendly Kittens is a feline leukemia negative colony, and our kittens are guaranteed free from genetic defects and PKD negative, so there is no need to worry about debilitating genetic problems.

Some of our kittens have even been adopted internationally — one flew to Venezuela on its first trip abroad. Ask me about the details if you are further afield.

My family and I love each kitten like a member of our family. You will not find a kitten more loved, more cared for, or better prepared to be adopted.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

How do I reserve a kitten?
Call or email and we will tell you what is available now and what is coming. Kittens are $1,400, with discounts for multiple kittens, cash referral bonuses, and a discount for teachers, firefighters, police, nurses and military. Most families book a visit first, meet the litter, and reserve the kitten they connect with.
When can my kitten come home?
Most kittens are adopted between eight and ten weeks old, once they are fully weaned and have had their first set of shots and their wellness check. Until then you are welcome to visit, and we will send photos as they grow.
How much grooming does a Himalayan need?
A long coat needs regular brushing, ideally a few minutes most days, to stay free of mats. Our kittens are brushed from the start, so they enjoy it rather than fight it, and we will show you how we do it.
Are they good with children and other pets?
Yes. Ours are raised around people, kids and other animals, so they do very well as lap cats and with small children, and they are very compatible with other cats and pets. They are not shy or unfriendly.
What paperwork comes with a kitten?
An adoption packet with a clean, signed bill of health from our local vet office — free of genetic defects, fleas, parasites and ringworm — plus the record of the first set of shots already given.
Can you help with spaying, neutering and rabies shots?
Yes. Because of our long relationship with our vet we can offer spay or neuter services, and the first rabies shot required by Virginia law, at steep discounts. Contact us with any questions about either.
Do you adopt outside Virginia?
We have — some of our kittens have been adopted internationally, including one that flew to Venezuela. Get in touch and we will talk through the details and what is possible.
