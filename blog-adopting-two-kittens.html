Should You Adopt Two Kittens? Littermates and Multi-Kitten Homes

Why two kittens can be easier than one, what to plan for, and our multiple-kitten discount.

By Sean Donovan, Himalayan and Persian breeder in Waterford, VA since 1995
KEY TAKEAWAYS
  • Two kittens keep each other company, play together and often settle faster.
  • Especially helpful if you are out of the house during the day.
  • Littermates already know each other.
  • Friendly Kittens offers a multiple-kitten discount.

Families often come to meet one kitten and leave thinking about two. Here is how to decide.

The case for two

  • Company while you are at work or out
  • They burn off kitten energy playing with each other, not your furniture
  • Littermates settle in together, with a familiar friend beside them
  • Two calm adults later, happy to share a lap

What to plan for

  • Double the food, litter and vet visits
  • At least one litter box per cat, plus one
  • Twice the brushing — a few minutes each most days
  • Two spay or neuter procedures, which we can offer at a discount through our vet

Common questions

Is it better to get two kittens?
For many homes, yes. Two kittens keep each other company and play together, which is especially helpful if you are out during the day.

Do you offer a discount for two kittens?
Yes. Friendly Kittens offers a multiple-kitten discount.

Should I get two kittens from the same litter?
Littermates already know each other, so they usually settle in together quickly.
